## Organization Agreement for SARbase

This Agreement is between **[OPERATOR LEGAL ENTITY — to be confirmed]**
("the Operator") and **[ORGANIZATION LEGAL NAME]** ("the
Organization"), governing the Organization's use of the SARbase
platform ("the Service").

### 1. Roles

1.1. The Operator provides hosted software that the Organization uses to
manage members, qualifications, equipment, communications, callouts,
incidents, and related records.

1.2. The Organization controls its workspace: which records it keeps,
who it authorizes, and how it uses the information. For personal data
in the workspace, the Organization is the data controller (or
equivalent role under applicable law); the Operator processes that
data on the Organization's documented instructions as described in
this Agreement and the Privacy Policy.

1.3. Individual users are additionally bound by the SARbase Terms of
Service; where this Agreement and the Terms conflict for the
Organization, this Agreement controls.

### 2. Service scope and operational independence

2.1. The Service is administrative, recordkeeping, and communication
software. It does not provide rescue services, emergency dispatch, or
operational command authority, and it does not certify personnel,
qualifications, equipment readiness, crew sufficiency, or the safety
of any operation.

2.2. The Organization retains exclusive responsibility for operational
decisions, emergency communications, compliance with applicable law,
training and safety standards, and the accuracy of records it
maintains in the Service.

2.3. Notifications (email, WhatsApp, or otherwise) traverse
third-party networks and may fail or be delayed. The Organization must
maintain independent emergency communication procedures and must not
represent Service notifications as guaranteed alerts.

### 3. Organization responsibilities

3.1. The Organization will: (a) ensure it has a lawful basis to submit
personal data about members, emergency contacts, and other
individuals; (b) keep member access, roles, and capabilities accurate
and revoke access promptly when people leave; (c) maintain accurate
records; (d) inform members that their data is processed in the
Service, including emergency-contact data about third parties; and
(e) respond to member privacy requests concerning workspace records.

3.2. The Organization will not use the Service unlawfully, submit data
it lacks the right to submit, or permit users to misuse the Service.

### 4. Operator responsibilities

4.1. The Operator will provide the Service with reasonable skill and
care, maintain the technical and organizational measures described in
the Privacy Policy (organization isolation, server-enforced
authorization, attachment malware scanning, audit logging), and
process workspace personal data only as needed to operate and secure
the Service or as required by law.

4.2. The Operator will use only the subprocessors listed in the
Privacy Policy (or a maintained equivalent list), remains responsible
for their performance, and will notify the Organization of material
changes to the subprocessor set.

4.3. The Operator will notify the Organization without undue delay
after confirming a security breach resulting in unauthorized access
to the Organization's workspace data, describing known scope and
remediation.

### 5. Data, export, and retention

5.1. Workspace records remain the Organization's property. The
Operator acquires no ownership and uses them only to operate the
Service.

5.2. The Organization can export its records through the Service's
export functions at any time.

5.3. The Operator retains data per the retention policy in the Privacy
Policy, including preservation of audit and change history. On
termination, workspace data remains available for export for
**[RETENTION WINDOW — pending legal decision]**, after which the
Operator may delete it except records required or justified to be
retained, which are retained only for that purpose.

### 6. Suspension and termination

6.1. Either party may terminate with **[NOTICE PERIOD]** written
notice. The Operator may suspend the Organization's workspace to
protect the Service or others where reasonably necessary, with notice
where practicable.

6.2. On termination the Organization is responsible for exporting the
records it requires during the export window.

7.2. The Organization's indemnity obligations mirror section 13 of the
